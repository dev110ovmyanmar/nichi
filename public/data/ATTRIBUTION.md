Vocabulary and kanji lists are derived from OpenJLPT
(https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), which incorporates:

- JMdict / EDICT and KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG)
  https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
- Jonathan Waller’s JLPT level assignments (tanos.co.uk, CC BY)
- Tatoeba example sentences (CC BY 2.0)
  https://tatoeba.org

Stroke-order diagrams come from KanjiVG (CC BY-SA 3.0)
https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg

The bundled OpenJLPT dataset is used under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The kanji *week/day shells* follow the pedagogical layout of
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Ask Publishing). Textbook lists and sentences are
not copied. https://www.ask-books.com/jlpt/n2/

Burmese explanations, grammar notes, reading passages, listening scripts,
mock-exam copy, and the Tango 2500 chapter-section shells in this app are
original Nichi content.
